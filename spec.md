We are making an interactive 3D landscape bathtub animation in Three.js

It takes a DEM and simulates rising flood waters from a river, and when they over top over levees, the water spills over into basins next to the river. The levees on one side of the river to another can have varying heights.

There will be a timeline scroll option (or just auto play) to control the time/flood level

As well as a "Flood gauge vertical metre" on screen

The overall project need to be under 100mb, including all assets like DEM etc
