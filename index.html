About this model ＋

The maximum flood envelope comes from the supplied 2022 d_Max and h_Max rasters, aligned from GDA94 to the GDA2020 terrain grid. Ground inside the wet extent is h_Max minus d_Max, which preserves both modelled depth and water elevation. The original survey DEM is retained outside that extent.

Each cell's maximum may occur at a different time. The envelope is shown at the gauge peak for comparison. Timing before and after it is interpolated from the approximate gauge trace. Floodgate and pump drainage is not represented. Terrain is shown at 4 m resolution with 1.5× vertical relief.
