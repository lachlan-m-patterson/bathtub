About this model ＋

The supplied DEM includes terrain and bathymetry. The peak extent and depths are the modelled 2022 flood (d_Max). Water rises and falls with the approximately traced gauge record, filling basins once a wet path connects them to the river. Ponded water can remain after the river recedes.

Timing before and after the peak is interpolated from the gauge, not modelled. Floodgate and pump drainage is not represented. The 4 m grid smooths features narrower than its cells. Vertical relief is exaggerated 1.5 times.
